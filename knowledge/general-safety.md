# General Workshop Safety (all shops)

Source keys: `LS` = Log Sheets ME-106.pdf; `CLLS` = Central Lathe Log sheet.pdf; `MLS1` = Milling Log Sheet #1.jpg; `ELS` = Electroplating Log Sheet.jpg; `TA` = Milling TA.pptx; `GPPT` = GrindingPPT.pptx; `PPT` = Fitting lab lecture final.pptx. `theory:` = Hajra Choudhury Vol. I (Safety in workshop); general workshop safety practice.

## Personal protective equipment and dress
- [SAF-F001] Wear the workshop apron in every shop. [LS p.1, p.4, p.7; MLS1; ELS; TA s.2]
- [SAF-F002] Wear closed safety (leather) shoes — they protect the feet from falling objects and hot chips. [LS p.7; MLS1; TA s.2; PPT s.13]
- [SAF-F003] Wear tight-fitting clothes; loose clothing can be caught by rotating parts. [LS p.1; CLLS p.1]
- [SAF-F004] Remove loose accessories — rings, bracelets, chains, wristwatches, loose threads. [LS p.7; TA s.2; GPPT s.2]
- [SAF-F005] Tie long hair back (or cover it). [LS p.7]
- [SAF-F006] Wear safety goggles where chips, sparks or splashes can fly (grinding, chipping, filing, plating). [PPT s.13; theory: Hajra Choudhury]
- [SAF-F007] Do not wear gloves near rotating parts (lathe chuck, milling cutter, drill) — they can be caught and pull the hand in. [theory: Hajra Choudhury]

## Machine operation
- [SAF-F008] Never operate a machine without the shop instructor's permission/supervision. [TA s.2; GPPT s.2]
- [SAF-F009] Stay alert and attentive while the machine is running; do not talk or fool around. [MLS1; TA s.2]
- [SAF-F010] Keep a safe distance from moving parts of a running machine. [MLS1; LS p.7; CLLS p.1]
- [SAF-F011] Remove the chuck key / spanners from the machine before switching on. [theory: Hajra Choudhury]
- [SAF-F012] Stop the machine before measuring, cleaning, adjusting or changing tools/speeds. [LS p.3; theory: Hajra Choudhury]
- [SAF-F013] Know the location of the emergency stop switch; switch off the machine in case of trouble. [theory: Hajra Choudhury]
- [SAF-F014] Never leave a running machine unattended. [theory: Hajra Choudhury]
- [SAF-F015] Clamp the job and tool firmly before starting. [LS p.7; MLS1; PPT s.13]
- [SAF-F016] Remove chips with a brush or chip hook, never with bare hands or by blowing. [LS p.1; theory: Hajra Choudhury]
- [SAF-F017] Do not touch electrical connections or switches with wet hands. [LS p.9; theory: Hajra Choudhury]
- [SAF-F018] Handle heavy machine parts and tools with care. [LS p.1; CLLS p.1]

## Housekeeping and general
- [SAF-F019] Keep the floor and work area clean and free of oil, chips and obstacles. [PPT s.13; TA s.2]
- [SAF-F020] Clean the machine and put tools back in their place after use. [TA s.2]
- [SAF-F021] Use the right tool for the job and never use damaged tools. [PPT s.13]
- [SAF-F022] Report every injury, however small, to the instructor; know where the first-aid box is. [theory: Hajra Choudhury]
- [SAF-F023] Fire-extinguisher classes: water for ordinary combustibles; CO₂ or dry powder for electrical and oil fires (never water on electrical or oil fires). [theory: general fire safety]
- [SAF-F024] Safety colour codes: red = stop/danger/fire equipment; yellow = caution; green = safe/first aid; blue = mandatory instruction. [theory: IS 9457 / ISO 3864]
- [SAF-F025] Safety = Skill + Awareness + Discipline. [PPT s.13]

## More workshop safety (standard practice)
- [SAF-F026] Lift heavy jobs by bending the knees and keeping the back straight; get help for heavy or awkward loads. [theory: general workshop safety]
- [SAF-F027] A minor burn should be cooled under clean running water for several minutes; do not apply grease or oil. [theory: first aid practice]
- [SAF-F028] If a chemical splashes into the eyes, rinse them at once with plenty of clean water (eye-wash) for at least 15 minutes and report it. [theory: lab safety]
- [SAF-F029] Never use compressed air to clean clothes or skin, or to blow chips towards people. [theory: general workshop safety]
- [SAF-F030] Keep gangways and emergency exits clear and know the exit route. [theory: general workshop safety]
- [SAF-F031] Never distract or startle someone who is operating a machine. [theory: general workshop safety]
