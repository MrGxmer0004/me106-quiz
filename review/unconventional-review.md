## Verified facts

- **Log sheet p.1, objectives:** study wire EDM; study the fiber laser cutting machine. ✓ (0068)
- **Log sheet p.1, jobs:** EDM square 10 mm × 10 mm, 5 mm thick (isometric sketch); laser ring with no dimensions. ✓ (0069–0072, 0146–0148)
- **Log sheet p.1, tools:** wire-cut EDM machine, clamping tool, clamping wire, power supply, dielectric fluid supply, part programming, wire electrode (7). ✓ (0073, 0074)
- **Log sheet p.1, procedure:** 11 numbered steps: clamp → fix and tension wire → set parameters → supply dielectric → enter profile by part programming → maintain spark gap → apply pulsed voltage → removal by spark heat → during Ton discharge removes material → wire moves along programmed profile and completes cut → remove and inspect. The order in 0075–0084 is correct; only the step numbers in 0082 and 0083 are off.
- **Log sheet p.2, laser procedure (7 steps):** clamp sheet → profile in CNC controller → power, speed, assist gas → focus through remote → dry cutting check → start beam and cut → remove and inspect. ✓ (0085–0092)
- **Log sheet p.2, precautions (6):** clamp workpiece; correct wire tension and spark gap; continuous dielectric; Ton, Toff and peak current; laser power and cutting speed; dry laser cutting check. ✓ (0093–0098)
- **Theory, checked against standard texts:**
  - Material is removed by pulsed sparks with no contact, and the work must be conductive.
  - A short circuit happens on contact. The servo holds a gap of about 0.025–0.075 mm.
  - Ton is when material is removed. Toff lets the dielectric de-ionise and the debris flush out.
  - Dielectric: deionised water for WEDM, hydrocarbon oil (kerosene) for die-sinking. Its functions are to insulate, concentrate the spark, cool and flush.
  - The wire is brass, about 0.05–0.3 mm, and used once.
  - Kerf = d + 2g, wire offset = r + g, and there is a recast layer over a HAZ.
  - Taper cuts come from offsetting the guides, and internal profiles need a start hole.
  - Fiber lasers use Yb-doped fiber at about 1.06 µm (CO₂ is 10.6 µm), and metals absorb 1 µm light better.
  - Assist gas: O₂ for mild steel (exothermic, oxide edge), N₂ for stainless steel and aluminium (clean edge), air for cheap cuts on thin sheet.
  - Dross is melt that re-solidifies on the bottom edge. Cutting too fast gives an incomplete cut and dross; too slow gives a wide kerf and HAZ.
  - The collimator makes the beam parallel and the focusing lens brings it to a spot. Stand-off is the gap from nozzle tip to work.
  - Cu and brass are reflective and hard to cut.
  - Energy classes: mechanical (USM, AJM, WJM), thermal (EDM, LBM, EBM, PAM), electrochemical (ECM, the reverse of plating), chemical.
