# Corrections to source material

Each item says what a source says, what the knowledge base uses instead, and why. No question uses the uncorrected value as its answer key.

| # | Source | What it says | What we use | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manufacturing notes p.16 | Medium-carbon steel 1.5–2% C; "high speed steel 2–5%" C | Low/mild < 0.3%, medium 0.3–0.6%, high 0.6–1.5% C; HSS is an alloy tool steel (~0.7–0.8% C + W/Cr/V) | Standard classification (Hajra Choudhury; Callister) |
| 2 | Manufacturing notes p.16 | "6.67% carbon → maximum possible carbon in steel" | Steel ≤ ~2.1% C; 6.67% C is cementite (Fe₃C); above ~2.1% is cast iron | Fe–C phase diagram (Callister) |
| 3 | Manufacturing notes p.3 | DP = no. of teeth / total circumference | DP = N / pitch-circle diameter (in) | Definition of diametral pitch (Machinery's Handbook; Milling TA slide 19) |
| 4 | Manufacturing notes p.4 | W.D. = 2.157/16 = 0.1346 in | 2.157/16 = 0.1348 in | Arithmetic |
| 5 | Milling Formula_.jpg & Milling Log Sheet #2 | 2.157/16 = 0.1328″ = 3.4 mm | 0.1348 in ≈ 3.42 mm (3.4 mm is still right) | Arithmetic slip on the board, copied onto the log sheet |
| 6 | Lab board / log sheets / notebook | "Working depth = 2.157/DP" | Kept as the lab's name for the depth you set.
